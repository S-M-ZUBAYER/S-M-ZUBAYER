# Hey, I'm Zubayer 👋

I build web apps and APIs, from the screens people use every day to the data and logic behind them. I enjoy taking busy, real-world workflows and making them easier to work with.

You'll find a bit of everything here: ERP and business tools, family and community apps, language and AI projects, and some earlier experiments. Have a look around!

## What I like building

- 🏭 **Tools for real work:** inventory, orders, task management, and service workflows. See my [THT Task Management System](https://github.com/S-M-ZUBAYER/THT-Task-Management-System) and [refund, repair & resend system](https://github.com/S-M-ZUBAYER/THT-Refund-Repair-Resend-Management-System).
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Spaces for people:** family coordination, learning, and communities. I have also worked on [ZUSS School Management](https://github.com/S-M-ZUBAYER/zuss-school-management-system) and [Study Zone](https://github.com/S-M-ZUBAYER/Study-Zone-platform).
- 🤖 **Language and AI:** translation, detection, chat, and practical API integrations. [ZUSS GPT](https://github.com/S-M-ZUBAYER/ZUSS-GPT) is another project in this area.
- 🛍️ **Commerce and content:** from an [online shop](https://github.com/S-M-ZUBAYER/Grozziee-Online-Shop) to a [news portal](https://github.com/S-M-ZUBAYER/focus-news-portal-clint).
